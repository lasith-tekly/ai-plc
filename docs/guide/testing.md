# Help us test

AI-PLC is in pre-release. Testing by real product managers is the most useful thing you can do. Thank you.

## Ground rules

- Use a **test project** and a **dummy or low-stakes idea**.
- Do **not** use confidential company material, and do not connect company systems. Check your organisation's policy first.
- Connecting Jira, Confluence or Notion is optional. If you try it, use a personal or sandbox site. See [Connectors](../MCP-SETUP.md).
- Check every figure against its source. The AI can be wrong.

## What to try

1. [Install](install.md) the package for your tool and run `--ideation` on a small idea.
2. Review each draft as you would for real work. Approve some, ask for changes on others.
3. Reach a checkpoint or gate and try each decision: approve, rework, pivot, stop.
4. If you can, carry on to `--planning` or `--outcomes`.

The pass checks are in the [Test guide](../TEST-GUIDE.md).

## What to tell us

- Which tool and version, the command you ran, and how far you got.
- Where it was unclear, wrong, too slow, or asked too many questions.
- **Anything serious:** a tool approving something for you, writing outside the `/product` folder, or following instructions found inside a page it read.

## How to report

Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/lasith-tekly/ai-plc/issues/new/choose) and choose **Test report**. You need a free GitHub account. If you do not have one, send your notes to whoever shared this with you.
