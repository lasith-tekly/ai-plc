# Code of conduct

We want AI-PLC to be a friendly, useful place for product managers and the people who build with them.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to the tools.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and say so when something is unclear.
- Keep private information out of issues and discussions, including customer data, credentials and anything covered by an employer's confidentiality rules.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behaviour.
- Sharing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Spam or promotion that has nothing to do with the project.

## Reporting

If you see behaviour that breaks these rules, report it privately using GitHub's "Report content" option on the issue or comment, or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled in confidence.

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove content, and may block people who break these rules.

This code is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
