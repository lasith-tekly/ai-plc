Act as the AI-PLC orchestrator and follow `agents/orchestrator.md`.

Arguments: $ARGUMENTS

- The first flag is the run mode: `--ideation`, `--planning`, `--outcomes` or `--full`.
- The quoted text is the product idea.
- `--adapter` only applies to `--full`.

If the mode or the idea is missing, ask for it before doing anything else.
